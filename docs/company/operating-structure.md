## Current v1 scope

The first authorized implementation is intentionally narrow:

- Constitution v1.0;
- GPT ↔ Claude Executive Operating Agreement v1.0;
- SOP-001 for bounded executive sessions;
- recommendation-only GPT/Claude sessions;
- creator ≠ approver;
- no model tools or production execution in executive sessions;
- explicit provider degradation;
- durable session audit events;
- Founder authority and existing workforce permission boundaries remain unchanged.

## Not yet implemented

The following remain later stages and must not be represented as complete:

- automatic executive-session triggering from workforce tasks;
- measured provider-health routing;
- canonical world-state evidence packages;
- Founder notification delivery;
- formal Founder Away Mode;
- automatic revenue operations;
- indefinite unattended operation.

## Long-term objective

Build toward founder-independent routine operations without becoming independent of Dwight's ownership, authority, standards, or right to intervene.

The company should use real financial, business, trading, product, revenue and operating data to optimize for Dwight's actual current stage while protecting against scope creep, fabricated certainty and reckless risk.
