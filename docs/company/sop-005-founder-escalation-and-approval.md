# SOP-005 — Founder Escalation & Approval

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** JARVIS / Chief of Staff  
**Authority:** HJV Constitution v1.0

## Purpose
Interrupt Dwight only for decisions that genuinely require Founder authority while keeping him fully in control.

## Escalate for
- new missions or material scope expansion;
- contracts or binding commitments;
- meaningful spending/capital outside approved budgets;
- consequential external communication;
- permissions/credential changes;
- high-blast-radius or difficult-to-reverse production changes;
- unresolved executive disagreement on a consequential decision;
- critical incidents;
- permanent authority expansion.

## Escalation packet
Every Founder request should contain:
1. what happened;
2. why Dwight is needed;
3. recommendation;
4. strongest alternative;
5. evidence;
6. downside / reversibility;
7. deadline, if real;
8. buttons/options: APPROVE, DENY, MODIFY, or DEFER where appropriate.

Do not send Dwight routine work that can safely be solved below him.

## Definition of done
Dwight's decision is recorded durably, the affected task resumes or closes, and the reason remains auditable.
